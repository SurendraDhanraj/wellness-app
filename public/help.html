Getting Started

Login, first-time setup, and navigating the app

Login screen

Logging In

1

Open the App

Visit the app URL provided by your administrator in any mobile or desktop browser.

2

Enter Credentials

Use the email address and temporary password sent to you by your administrator.

3

Set Your Password

On first login you will be prompted to choose a new personal password. Your password must be kept confidential.

4

Complete Your Profile

Fill in your personal details, organisational info (Business Unit, Department, Location), and optionally upload a profile photo.

Forgot your password? Tap Forgot Password on the login screen and submit a reset request. An administrator will approve it and send a recovery code.

Activities & Enrollments

Browse, enrol, submit proof, and earn points

Activities list

Submitting proof

Browsing Activities

Tap Activities in the bottom nav to see all available wellness activities. Use the category filter tabs to browse by type:

🏃 Physical 👥 Social 💰 Financial 🧘 Emotional

Each activity card shows the activity name, category, point value, and duration. Tap a card to see full details.

Enrolling & Submitting Proof

Activity detail & Enrol

Submit proof

1

Tap Enrol

Open an activity and tap Enrol Now. Your status moves to In Progress.

2

Complete the Activity

Participate in the activity at your own pace.

3

Submit Proof

Tap Submit Proof on the activity. Upload a photo, video, or document, add an optional note, then tap Submit. Status becomes Pending Verification.

4

Earn Points

Once an admin verifies your submission, status changes to Verified and points are added to your total automatically.

Enrollment Statuses

StatusMeaning
In ProgressYou are enrolled and actively working on the activity
Pending VerificationProof submitted — awaiting admin review
VerifiedApproved — points have been added to your account
RejectedSubmission was not accepted — see the admin note for details

Health Tracker

Log and monitor your personal health metrics

Health tracker

Logging Health Data

Tap Health in the bottom nav. Tap + Log Today to add a new entry. You can log:

⚖️

Weight

Enter your weight in kg or lbs.

📊

BMI

Body Mass Index — calculated or entered manually.

❤️

Blood Pressure

Systolic / Diastolic readings in mmHg.

👟

Steps

Daily step count.

Past entries are listed in reverse-chronological order. Tap an entry to view details. Your dashboard automatically pulls from the most recent entry.

Privacy: Your health data is only visible to you and authorised administrators. It is never shared publicly.

Administrator Guide

Managing the Heritage Wellness platform

Administrators have access to a dedicated admin interface. After logging in, admins are taken to the Admin Dashboard instead of the employee view.

Admin Navigation: Dashboard · Health · Manage · Bulletin · Settings

Admin dashboard

The admin dashboard provides organisation-wide KPIs:

Verifying Activity Submissions

Review and approve or reject employee proof

Verify submissions

Tap Pending on the dashboard, or go to Admin → Verify. Each submission card shows:

  • Employee name and the activity they completed
  • Uploaded proof (image, video, or document)
  • Employee's optional note
  • Submission timestamp

Approving a Submission

1

Review the proof

Tap the attachment to view the uploaded evidence in full.

2

Tap Verify

Points are automatically added to the employee's account. They receive a notification.

3

Or tap Reject

Enter an optional note explaining why, then confirm. The employee is notified and can re-submit.

Note: Verifying a submission awards the activity's full point value. Deleting a verified enrollment later will automatically deduct those points.
❤️

Health & Enrollment Management

View, edit, and manage employee health data and enrollments

Health & Enrolled tabs

Go to Health in the admin nav. This page has two tabs:

Health Tab

  1. Search for an employee by name or email
  2. Tap their name to view all their health metric entries
  3. Tap Edit on any entry to correct data
  4. Tap Delete to permanently remove an entry

Enrolled Tab

Shows all activity enrollments across the entire organisation with filters and bulk actions:

  • Search by employee name, email, or activity name
  • Filter by status (In Progress, Pending, Verified, Rejected)
  • Delete individual — tap the trash icon on a card
  • Bulk delete — tap Select in the header, check cards, then Delete selected
  • Delete all — deletes all records matching the current filter/search
Important: Deleting a verified enrollment automatically deducts the associated points from the employee's total.
📌

Bulletin Board Moderation

Moderate employee posts to maintain a safe environment

Go to Bulletin in the admin nav. All posts are displayed with moderation options:

ActionEffect
🗑️ DeletePermanently removes the post. Employee is not notified.
🚫 CensorHides the post content and replaces it with a moderation message. The post record is retained.
↩️ RestoreUn-censors a previously censored post, making it visible again.
Best Practice: Use Censor when you may need to review further. Use Delete for clear policy violations.

Managing Activities

Create, edit, and deactivate wellness activities

Go to Manage in the admin nav.

Creating a New Activity

1

Tap + New Activity

Opens the activity creation form.

2

Fill in Details

Enter name, description, category, points value, optional duration, and an emoji icon.

3

Tap Save

The activity is immediately available for employees to enrol in.

Editing an Activity

Tap the Edit (pencil) icon on any activity card. Update any fields and tap Save. Changes take effect immediately.

Deactivating an Activity

Toggle the active switch on an activity card to deactivate it. Deactivated activities are hidden from employees but existing enrollments are preserved.

User & Password Management

Invite employees and handle account access

Inviting a New Employee

1

Go to Settings → Invite User

Enter the employee's email address and select their role (Employee or Admin).

2

Send Invite

A temporary password is generated. Share it securely with the employee.

3

Employee logs in

They will be prompted to set a new password and complete their profile on first login.

Password Reset Requests

When an employee submits a password reset request, it appears in the Pending counter on the admin dashboard. To approve:

  1. Go to Admin → Verify and switch to the Password Resets section
  2. Review the request and tap Approve Reset
  3. A recovery code is issued to the employee

Deactivating a User

Go to Settings → Manage Admins or the user management section. Toggle the user's active status off. Deactivated users cannot log in but their data is preserved.

⚙️

Configuration

Set up Business Units, Departments, and Locations

Go to Settings → Configuration to manage the organisational structure used across the app.

🏢

Business Units

Top-level organisational divisions. Employees are assigned to one Business Unit during profile setup.

🗂️

Departments

Sub-groups within the organisation. Used for leaderboard filtering and reporting.

📍

Locations

Office or work sites. Employees select their primary location on their profile.

Adding / Editing

  1. Select the category tab (Business Units, Departments, or Locations)
  2. Tap + Add New, enter the name, and tap Save
  3. To edit, tap the pencil icon on any existing entry
  4. To deactivate, toggle the active switch — deactivated items no longer appear in dropdowns
Caution: Deactivating a Business Unit, Department, or Location does not remove it from existing user profiles. It only prevents new users from selecting it.
